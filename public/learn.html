Overview

The One Piece Card Game is a 1-on-1 trading card game published by Bandai. Each player commands a Leader — a famous face from the series — and builds a 50-card deck around them, playing Character, Event, and Stage cards to whittle the opponent's Life down to zero.

A distinctive part of the game is the DON!! deck: a resource system (10 cards per player) that both pays for your cards and can be attached directly to your Leader or Characters for a power boost during your turn. Mastering DON!! timing is most of what separates a good player from a great one.

This simulator automates all the bookkeeping — shuffling, drawing, DON!!, life, and combat math — so you can focus on strategy. Card Types

Leader
One per deck, always in play face-up. Sets your deck's color(s) and starting Life total, and usually has its own ability.
Character
The bulk of your deck. Played from hand for their cost, they sit in your Character Area (max 5 at once) and can attack or block.
Event
One-time effects. Play them from hand during your Main Phase, or use their [Counter] text during a battle — then they go to the trash.
Stage
Persistent field cards (only 1 at a time) that usually grant an ongoing or activatable effect.

Every card also has a color (Red, Green, Blue, Purple, Black, or Yellow — some Leaders are two colors), a cost, and Characters/Leaders have power. Many Characters also print a counter value you can pay by discarding them from hand during a battle for a temporary power boost.

The Board

Each player's side of the table has these zones:

Deck50 cards, face-down, secret. Draw from the top.
DON!! Deck10 generic DON!! cards — a shared, open resource, not part of your 50-card deck.
HandCards you've drawn; secret from your opponent.
Life AreaA face-down stack equal to your Leader's Life value. Secret to both players until revealed by damage.
Cost AreaWhere active DON!! cards wait to be spent or attached.
Leader AreaYour Leader card, always face-up in play.
Character AreaUp to 5 Character cards in play at once.
Stage Area1 Stage card at a time.
TrashFace-up discard pile for K.O.'d Characters and used Events.

Game Setup

  1. Both players shuffle their 50-card deck.
  2. Take cards from the top of your deck equal to your Leader's Life value and place them face-down as your Life area (don't look at them!).
  3. Draw a 5-card starting hand.
  4. Mulligan (optional, once): you may shuffle your starting hand back into your deck and draw a fresh 5.
  5. Randomly determine who goes first (this simulator flips a coin for you). The first player skips their first Draw Phase and only gets 1 DON!! card on their first turn instead of 2 — a small tradeoff for having the first move.

Turn Structure

Every turn moves through five phases, in order:

1
Refresh
All your rested cards become active again, and all DON!! you gave to your Leader/Characters return to your Cost Area.
2
Draw
Draw 1 card (skipped for the first player's very first turn).
3
DON!!
Add 2 DON!! cards (1 on the first player's turn 1) to your Cost Area, active.
4
Main
Play cards, attach DON!!, activate abilities, and attack — in any order, as many times as you can afford.
5
End
"End of turn" effects resolve, then priority passes to your opponent.

The DON!! System

DON!! cards are the game's engine. Every turn you gain DON!! cards into your Cost Area, active side up. From there, during your Main Phase, you can:

  • Pay for cards: rest a number of active DON!! equal to a card's cost to play it.
  • Attach DON!! for power: move an active DON!! card from your Cost Area underneath your Leader or a Character. Each attached DON!! grants +1000 power for the rest of your turn. At the start of your next turn (Refresh Phase) all given DON!! return to your Cost Area, active — so attaching is a per-turn decision, not a permanent buff.

Many cards also have a [DON!! x1] / [DON!! x2] style effect that only turns on while you have that many DON!! attached to that card — a big part of deckbuilding is deciding how aggressively to attach DON!! for stats versus holding it back to pay for more plays.

Battle

During your Main Phase you may attack with your Leader and/or any of your active Characters that aren't already rested (and don't have "summoning sickness" — see Rush below). A battle has four steps:

  1. Attack Step: rest the attacking Leader/Character and declare a target — your opponent's Leader, or one of their Rested Characters. (Active Characters are normally safe from attack — that's what makes Blocker special.)
  2. Block Step: the defender may rest an active Character with [Blocker] to redirect a Leader attack onto it instead, even though it wasn't a legal target on its own.
  3. Counter Step: the defender may play [Counter] Events from hand, or discard Characters for their printed counter value, to boost the defending side's power for this battle only.
  4. Damage Step: compare power. If the attacker's power is greater than or equal to the defender's, the attacker wins: a Character target is K.O.'d (sent to the trash), or if the Leader was hit, the defender takes damage — the top card of their Life area moves to their hand (see Trigger below). If the attacker's power is lower, nothing happens; the attacking card just stays rested.

Keywords

[Rush]
This Character can attack the same turn it's played, ignoring the usual "summoning sickness."
[Blocker]
May rest itself during the Block Step to become the new target of an attack, protecting the original target.
[Double Attack]
If this card wins a battle against the Leader, it deals 2 Life damage instead of 1.
[Banish]
When this card deals Life damage, the revealed Life card is trashed instead of added to hand — its [Trigger] can't be used.
[Trigger]
Printed on some cards. When one is revealed by taking damage, its owner may activate the printed Trigger effect instead of adding it to hand.
[Counter]
Can be played from hand during the Counter Step (Events), or is a power value discardable from hand during a battle (Characters).

Deck Building

  • Choose exactly 1 Leader card. It sets the color(s) your deck can use.
  • Build a 50-card main deck using only Character, Event, and Stage cards that share at least one color with your Leader.
  • No more than 4 copies of any card with the same card number (alternate art printings of the same card still count together).
  • Your DON!! deck is a fixed set of 10 generic DON!! cards — you don't build it, it's the same for every deck.

Winning & Losing

You lose the game immediately if either of these happens:

  • Your Leader takes Life damage while you have 0 Life cards remaining.
  • You're required to draw a card but your deck is empty.

There's no separate "win" condition to track — just avoid both of the above longer than your opponent does.

Tips for New Captains

  • Don't over-attach DON!!. Power is great, but DON!! attached to a Character is DON!! you can't spend on new cards or your Leader's abilities that turn.
  • Watch your opponent's Life count. Cards get much stronger ("if you have 2 or less Life cards…") as a player gets low — don't assume a losing board position is safe to press.
  • Blockers buy tempo, not always value. Trading a 1-cost Blocker to save your Leader 5000 power of damage is usually worth it.
  • Count your outs before attacking. If your opponent has open DON!! and cards in hand, assume they have a Counter or Blocker ready.
